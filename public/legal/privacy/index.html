Privacy Policy — SMM Stats

Last updated: November 9, 2026

SMM Stats is a private analytics tool. It is used only by its owner to collect statistics about the owner's own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Threads). It is not offered to the public.

What data we access

After the account owner signs in and grants permission, the service reads: basic profile information (display name, avatar, follower count) and public statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares), and the text of comments under those videos to count a code word.

How we use it

The data is used only to build statistics reports for the account owner. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens and aggregated statistics are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV. Comment texts are not stored: only the number of matching comments is kept.

Deleting data

You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok, Instagram or Google account settings. To delete stored data, email the contact below; it will be removed within 7 days.
